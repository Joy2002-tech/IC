About & Founder

The Person Behind Igris Capital

Founded on the belief that every person deserves clear, honest financial guidance without jargon, without pressure, without conflict of interest.

Joy Mendonsa
Founder & Principal Advisor · Igris Capital

Joy Mendonsa is the founder and principal advisor at Igris Capital a boutique capital and wealth advisory practice based in Mira Bhayandar, Mumbai. With a focused approach to financial planning, Joy works directly with every client no handoffs, no junior advisors, no call centres.

Igris Capital was built on a single principle: that financial advice should be honest, coordinated, and genuinely in the client's interest. Joy operates across loans, mutual fund distribution, insurance, and tax support structuring all four into one coherent strategy for each client's life goals.

Having worked with clients across professions salaried professionals, business owners, and families Joy brings a practical, grounded approach to what can often be an overwhelming landscape of financial products and advice.
